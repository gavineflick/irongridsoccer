🔒 Grid locked — numbers randomly assigned. Find the winner by matching the last digit of each score to the grid axes.
Home team → columns (last digit)  |  Away team → rows (last digit)

How to find the winner: Take the last digit of the home team’s score — find that column. Take the last digit of the away team’s score — find that row. The square where they meet wins that checkpoint’s points.

Square distribution: Each entrant receives squares proportional to their entry points. If the total doesn’t divide evenly into 100 squares, remaining squares are assigned randomly.

Scoring checkpoints: 1st Quarter, Halftime, 3rd Quarter, and Final Score. Points are awarded to the owner of the winning square at each checkpoint.
